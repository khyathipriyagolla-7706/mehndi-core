Our story

Traditional art, brought to your door

Mehndi Core is a home mehndi service based in Eluru, built around one goal: making beautiful, professional henna art accessible without the travel, waiting rooms, or crowded salon chairs. Whether it's a full bridal sitting or a quick design before a festival, our artists bring their own supplies and set up right in your home.

Every appointment is booked directly through WhatsApp, so you can confirm your date, ask questions, and share reference designs with a real person — not a call centre.
